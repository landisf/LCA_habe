* Reviewer 1
/Referee report on Florian Landis, "Consumption based responsibility/
/for global warming and the distribution of impacts of Swiss climate/
/policy" The author combines the Swiss Household budget survey data of/
/2015-2017, the last full set available today, with third-party data/

/dataset to compute the greenhouse gas footprint of households. This is/
/the "consumption based responsibility for global warming" of the/
/title. Next, he looks for regularities in these data, in particular/
/for a relationship between GHG footprint and income. Third, he/
/examines how existing Swiss climate policy could burden the households/
/by income class through its impact on the prices of the spending/
/items. This is "the distribution of impacts of Swiss climate policy"/
/of the title. Last, he simulates a uniform tax on all GHG emissions/
/and checks again how this burden the households by income class,/
/taking into account the uniform refunding of the revenues of the tax./

/What is interesting in this paper:/
- /At least since the yellow vest movement and the rise in energy/
  /poverty, policy makers have become aware that they need to know who/
  /is most affected by climate policies. To the extent that policies/
  /can be fine-tuned, even a carbon or GHG tax, such information can/

  /information can guide the design of social-cushioning measures./

  /footprint with household type, in particular income class, it does/

  /studies. One learns a lot on this from this analysis./

  /expenditure items – how each policy feature could make these items/
  /more expensive – and then showing how this affects different/
  /household types is very attractive./
